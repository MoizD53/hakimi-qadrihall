Terms & Conditions

Please read and agree to all venue rules and booking policies before proceeding to view the halls and submit booking enquiries.

✦

1. Permission

All functions, Majlis, and events require permission and approval from Aamil Saheb.

2. Booking Confirmation

Submitting an enquiry does not confirm a booking. Confirmation is subject to availability and written approval by the manager.

3. Payment Terms

A 40% non-refundable advance is required after booking approval by management. The remaining 60% must be paid at least 2 days before the event date.

4. Hall Availability

Our venue offers two banquet halls: Evan-e-Hakimi (2nd Floor) is open for current bookings, and Qadri Hall (3rd Floor) will be available for bookings from 15th Nov 26.

5. Capacity

Approximate capacity is 35–40 thals for dining and seating.

6. Timings

Events must end and the hall must be vacated within the booked slot (Morning: 10:30 AM – 2:30 PM, Evening: 6:30 PM – 10:30 PM, Full Day: 10:00 AM – 10:00 PM). Additional charges may apply for late departure.

7. Noise & Safety

All guests must follow local noise restrictions, fire-safety rules, and applicable laws.

8. Cleanliness & Damage

The event organizer must maintain cleanliness and may be responsible for damage caused to venue property.

9. Dress Code

Wearing Libas-ul-Anwar is compulsory for all guests attending the function.

10. Parking

Guests must not block entrances, driveways, or neighboring properties.

11. Cancellation

Cancellation, rescheduling, and refund requests are subject to the applicable booking agreement and management's confirmed policy.

12. Changes to Terms

Management reserves the right to update these Terms & Conditions when necessary.

Agreement Required
